## Lessons Learned

- A field agent's causal attribution deserves a source check before it becomes record. The
  de-advertise claim was internally plausible, was contradicted by the agent's own later entry, and
  would have produced an issue against the wrong repo. Two config lists with similar contents
  (`topics_list` at `:410-411` versus the persisted definitions at `:524-525`) is what made it
  convincing — reading either alone supports a different conclusion than reading both.
- Periods where autonomy is abandoned for manual control are invisible to an agent watching topics,
  and are exactly the periods most worth recording. Nothing in the stack marks the transition in a
  way the logs picked up.
- A capability stood down "temporarily" for link budget needs an owner and a restore trigger, or it
  becomes permanent silently. `manda_coverage_swath` has been off since 08-25 without the operator
  knowing.
- Sub-window tile publishing (#112) delivered its claimed saving under real survey load — 14.4 to
  16.8 percent of whole-tile cost, dropping to 0.1 percent over quiet ground.
- The mercat host participates in failures (its disk exhaustion stopped the sidescan) but has no
  way to contribute to the record. On 08-28 its log was relayed by hand into gabby's.
