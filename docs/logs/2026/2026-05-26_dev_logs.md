- Gating behaves as configured: SLOWDOWN → gated output ≈ 0.30× commanded (matches `slowdown_ratio`), STOP → output 0.00. Held across dozens of engagements at approach speeds up to **~2.57 m/s (~5 kt)**.

Mitigations to scope: **stop-latch / obstacle-memory**, near-field coverage, speed cap vs. coast-down.
