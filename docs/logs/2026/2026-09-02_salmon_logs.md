**2026-09-02 19:42 -04:00** — CORRECTION to the 19:40 design note (operator caught it): inter-pass residuals are NOT contaminated by water-level change — soundings are ellipsoid-referenced through GNSS nav, so drawdown drops out of pass-to-pass comparison entirely; the earlier 'must be removed from residuals' phrasing was wrong. Water level matters to the SS estimator as the VERTICAL COORDINATE CONVERSION, not a residual correction: stratification is surface-referenced (thermocline at depth-below-surface) while the store is ellipsoidal, so the water-level record is required to apply a profile across days (post-drawdown the same thermocline sits 0.74 m lower in ellipsoidal terms), to merge/refine profiles in a common frame, and to know column length / transducer start depth. Corollary: a bulk day-to-day vertical offset in residuals implicates GNSS/geoid, draft, or real bottom change — never water level. Water-level layer stays in the design, with this corrected rationale.
