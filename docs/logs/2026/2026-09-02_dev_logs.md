## Lessons Learned

- **The field agents corrected themselves four times in one day, in place, on the record** — the "no
  store depth at all" claim, the voided lake-gauge cross-check, the water-level-in-residuals error, and
  the mag-map v1 hotspots revised from 4000-6200 nT down to 414-475 nT. Two of those came from operator
  prompts. The pattern to keep is that each correction was written as a superseding entry naming what
  it replaced, rather than the original being edited away.
- **A recommendation for a safety-relevant config change outran its own analysis by thirty minutes.**
  The flip was recommended at 19:19; the finding that bounds what it can achieve landed at 19:49. The
  decision held up, but the sequence is the lesson: trace the consumer's code path *before*
  recommending a change to what it consumes, not after.
- **Self-consistency is not verification.** The camera TF check compared `/tf_static` against the URDF
  it is generated from. The agent said so plainly and then proposed the real test, which is the right
  shape for a check that can only ever detect a broken chain.
- **A capture can be clean and still be weak.** The calibration bag verified perfectly on frame counts
  and topic coverage, but 55% of its samples sit in one heading bin — a lawnmower survey does not sweep
  compass. Worth assessing capture *geometry* against the analysis's requirements while there is still
  time on the water to fix it.
- **`make sync` rebases, so a deliberate merge topology on a field repo will not survive it.** Verified
  in `sync_repos.py`; nothing was lost, but anyone relying on merge structure should know.
- pre-commit is not installed as a hook on **either** field clone. Both ran the suite by hand and
  passed; `--no-verify` was never used.
