## Lessons Learned

- **In a pinch, the boat can be driven on the segmentation streams alone.** While
  troubleshooting the network issues, the RGB camera streams were stopped but the
  segmentation streams were kept running. In a hover, the segmentation gave enough
  situational reassurance that there was no need to rush the RGB feeds back up —
  worth remembering as a low-bandwidth fallback for both bandwidth crunches and
  over-horizon ops.
