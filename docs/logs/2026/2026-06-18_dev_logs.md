## Lessons Learned

- **Weather / ops-stop is the operator's call, not the agent's to order.** The deployment agent repeatedly wrote "hard stop" / "TAKE SHELTER NOW" and ordered an ops-stop on the weather watches; the operator overrode with on-scene judgment and managed it safely (timed dock return). Per the urgency contract — risk-acceptance gates are the operator's, and suggestions are conversation — the agent should **advise** on weather, not **command** an ops-stop.
- **FCU USB flap = physical link, motion-correlated.** Reseat resolved it (provisional); consider strain-relief / cable-connector hardening on the Cube USB run.
