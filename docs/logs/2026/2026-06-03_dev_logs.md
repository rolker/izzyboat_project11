## Lessons Learned

- **Autonomous obstacle avoidance stays the fallback** (low-speed + manual override), not relied on
  with students — reinforced by the survey-speed behavior this day.
- **Do not attempt bridges** — both control modes fail at the span (autonomy stops, manual loses
  comms), and the boat drifts on comms loss. A failsafe gap (drift vs station-keep) to close.
- **Tune perception thresholds underway, not hovering** — hovering over-confirms marks.
- **Tracking quality is confounded by speed *and* sea-state** — can't separate from this day's runs;
  needs a controlled sheltered-vs-exposed test. The 360-loop instability (nav#66) is the real
  line-following blocker and is location-independent (will follow to the lake).
- **Endurance is voltage/model-based** (no current sensor) — plan to the conservative end with a
  return reserve; ~14 km / 4.8 h ≈ half a fresh charge, and Lake Massabesic should do better
  (gentler water, no current, once nav#66 is fixed). Recharge-to-full, not range, is the binding
  cadence constraint.

### Power / endurance analysis *(done 2026-06-04, voltage-based — no current sensor; analysis in `~/data/logs/analysis/2026-06-04/`)*

#211 was the **furthest/longest run to date** and a useful power data point (feeds
[`#196`](https://github.com/rolker/unh_echoboats_project11/issues/196) /
[`#167`](https://github.com/rolker/unh_echoboats_project11/issues/167)). All numbers
voltage/PWM-modeled — there is no current sensor (`BatteryState.current` is a placeholder).

- **Run scale:** 13.89 km of track, max **2131 m** from launch, 4.76 h in-water, mean
  1.0 m/s. Voltage **28.5 → 25.6 V** (open→close), **min 23.89 V** under heavy throttle —
  a fresh charge taken across the whole LiFePO₄ plateau to the **top of the knee** in one
  session (deepest single fresh-charge draw yet; prior fresh runs barely left the plateau).
- **Modeled ending SoC ≈ half remaining.** Coulomb model (idle + prop current over `rc/out`
  ch-0 ÷ 273 Ah): **~37 % (linear) to ~50 % (quadratic) remaining** at the 8 A idle anchor;
  ~39–52 % with the revised **6.2 A** idle (operator charger reading, all systems on ≈ idle —
  the model's 8 A anchor was ~23 % high, reinforcing the known over-prediction → true remaining
  likely ≥ 50 %).
- **Turning overhead quantified.** Reprojecting prop energy as *if it had tracked clean*
  (clean-cruise = straight, survey-speed, low-yaw samples; median PWM 1765): only **17 % of
  moving time was clean cruise**; the turning/correction overhead (the
  [`nav#66`](https://github.com/rolker/unh_marine_navigation/issues/66) instability) was
  **~21 Ah (lin) to ~38 Ah (quad) ≈ 13–29 % of consumption** — clean-tracking ending SoC
  would've been ~47–66 % remaining. The clean-cruise figure **still includes the Gulf chop**,
  so Lake Massabesic (sheltered, no tidal current) would be **better still**.
- **Takeaway:** #211's ~half-charge is a **pessimistic upper bound** for an equivalent lake
  survey — lower real idle + fixing nav#66 + gentler water all push endurance up. ~14 km / 4.8 h
  ≈ half a fresh charge sets a conservative leg-sizing envelope; recharge-to-full (not range)
  remains the binding cohort-cadence constraint.
