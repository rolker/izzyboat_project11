## Summary

The takeaway from this run is a hard problem, not a checklist: **turning a camera
image into a reliable costmap is hard.** Planned as a clean survey run (override/
resume #52, turning limits, cross-track), it became a perception session against a
3-buoy mooring field — and the segmentation→costmap→avoidance pipeline never became
trustworthy. The costmap timed out and flickered (`Costmap timed out` ×14), the
corridor avoider couldn't act on it (#57), and **the boat never went around the buoys
on its own** — handled by ~20 brief manual takeovers plus a few gentle bumps.

The basics held: override/resume largely validated (updatable goto ✓, updatable
hover ✓, mission re-send/adjust ✓; only mid-line resume failed), and line-following
stayed **sub-metre under heavy crab** (|XTE| median 0.47 m). In-water 13:37:27–
16:28:54 EDT (171 min). CAMP crash-loop fixed in the field (camp #58); the CA
slowdown field-tune was reverted; costmap/avoider issues filed (nav #56/#57). Turning
limits untested (the velocity_smoother is out of the cmd_vel path — seafloor #36).
