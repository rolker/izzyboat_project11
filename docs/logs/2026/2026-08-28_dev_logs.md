## Lessons Learned

- **A Windows reboot cost 53 minutes of sonar, and about 13 of those were not boot time.** Operator:
  *"The frustrating thing about mercat is that a windows reboot almost cost an hour of down time!"*
  Timeline: hang 10:24:50, reboot 10:29:56, OS answering ping ~10:45, RDP available 10:58:29, M3
  verified 11:12. The 10:45→10:58:29 gap was a service dependency failure that does not self-heal —
  Remote Desktop Services failed because State Repository Service hung in start-pending, and
  `TermService` reported Running while holding no listener on 3389. Two separable problems: the M3
  lockup, and a recovery path that needs a human to restart a service.
- **A host that participates in failures but cannot contribute to the record is a structural gap.**
  mercat caused the 08-26 sidescan outage (disk exhaustion) and owned the 08-28 M3 outage, yet both
  days its evidence reached the record only second-hand or by hand-carry.
- **The battery is the campaign-limiting resource, and currents dominate it.** Three consecutive days
  ended on battery: 08-27 shortened, 08-28 governing both the survey cutoff and the transit. The
  harbour entrance, not the open-water crossing, is where the margin went.
- **Deferring an analysis with a search pointer works.** The hover t0 was left for post-deployment
  with the log recording where to look, rather than being computed under sterile-cockpit pressure.
