## Summary

**The headline: BizzyBoat went around a moored sailboat on its own — the first
on-water autonomous obstacle avoidance.** The sea-surface costmap marked the boat,
the AvoidanceController bent the survey path around it, and the loosened smoother
passed the maneuver cleanly (14:25). Also confirmed this run: costmap-over-bridge to
CAMP without the rqt_udp_bridge hack (#56 / PR #61), the segmentation-timestamp fix
(#28), and sensor readiness for student sonar setup.

The flip side — **the boat couldn't yet run clean *straight* lines, and we found
why.** Two problems stacked. (1) The re-enabled velocity_smoother's tight yaw-accel
(±0.5) fought the directly-tuned PID → hunting; fixed on the water by raising it to
±3.0 (cross-track median 0.94→0.48 m; seafloor#38). (2) Underneath that, a
**persistent swerving** — heading reversing ~10× more than the prior run —
root-caused post-deployment to **the same AvoidanceController (#206) weaving the line
along the charted pier's 150 m inflation gradient** (the boat worked entirely inside
that halo; not camera false-positives). Filed **nav#63**, fix open. So the avoider is
both this run's milestone *and* its line-following problem.

A 15:27 **near-miss / manual takeover** showed turn-before-slowdown is speed-limited
at ~1.3 m/s (**nav#64**); OTH was deferred (#130). In-water 13:19:32–15:41:06
(141.6 min). Sensor/sonar readiness met for the teaching goal (QINSy recorded; data
offload + 1PPS confirmation pending).

## Lessons Learned

- **Re-enabling a component makes it the prime suspect** for any new misbehavior —
  lead with the smoother (#36); don't chase current→PID→wrapper first (~35 min lost).
- **Putting the avoider in the line-following loop (#206) means costmap content now
  perturbs every line** — a 150 m chart-inflation halo near the pier makes the boat
  swerve where the decoupled follower ran straight. An architecture decision with a cost.
- **Turn-before-slowdown trades safety margin for avoider freedom** — only safe when
  the boat can out-turn the obstacle (low speed); at survey speed the margin isn't optional.
- **Recover prior-run params by direct lookup, not bag reverse-engineering.**
- **Bag-derived times are authoritative** — live-ops stamps drifted ~25 min late under
  permission-prompt friction (logged ~13:44 vs 13:19:32 actual).
- When chasing a "frozen display," **measure bandwidth not just rate, and "what
  changed" (the #206 viz delta) beats "what's biggest" (the costmap).**
