**2026-08-04 14:09 -04:00** — ROOT CAUSE of the speed-dependent turn loss is MIXER HEADROOM, not hydrodynamics. Max available differential collapses linearly with throttle: throttle ~0us -> 1000us differential; 150-250 -> 700; 250-350 -> 500; 350-450 -> 298; 450-600 -> 100. That matches the pure saturation ceiling diff_max = 1000 - 2*throttle exactly, i.e. ArduPilot is CLIPPING steering rather than backing off throttle to preserve it. Meanwhile yaw rate achieved PER UNIT of differential is roughly flat across speed (0.19-0.28 rad/s per full differential at every band), so the hull's turning response is not what degrades — the boat simply is not being given the differential at speed. MOT_STR_THR_MIX (currently 0.5, skid-steer-only steering-vs-throttle prioritisation) is the knob that governs this and is the highest-value thing to test next.
