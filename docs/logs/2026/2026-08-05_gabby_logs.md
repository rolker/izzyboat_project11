**2026-08-05 16:12 -04:00** — KEYFRAME STRUCTURAL RISK + a config premise that needs checking. The bridge has no keyframe concept; it fragments messages. avg_fragment_count 13-14 is the MEAN across I and P frames, and an H265 IDR is several times the size of a P-frame, so a keyframe spans proportionally more fragments. Losing any ONE fragment loses the WHOLE frame (the defragmenter cannot complete it), so per-frame loss probability scales with fragment count — keyframes are structurally the most likely frames to be lost AND the most damaging, since the decoder cannot recover until the next IDR.
